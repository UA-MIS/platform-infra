Platform tools

Everything the platform runs, in one place. If you've never used one of these before, the one-liner under each name tells you what it's for — click through and you'll already be signed in.

Build & ship

Backstage
The developer portal — onboard a new app, manage its secrets, and browse the catalog of everything running on the platform. Start here.
process.capstone.uamishub.com
Harbor
The container image registry — where your app's Docker images land after CI builds them, and get pulled from when deployed.
harbor.capstone.uamishub.com
Argo CD
GitOps deployment — shows exactly what's deployed right now and keeps it in sync with what's committed to git.
argocd.capstone.uamishub.com

Observe & debug

Grafana
Dashboards & logs — metrics, alerts, and log search for your app and the platform it runs on.
grafana.capstone.uamishub.com
Your database console
Browse and query your team's database directly. Every team gets its own console at <team>-db.capstone.uamishub.com — find your team's exact link on your app's page in Backstage (Overview tab), where you're already signed in.
DB Admin (platform team)
The platform-wide admin console, listing every platform database (Backstage, Harbor, Crossplane, MariaDB). UA-MIS platform team (labmx) only — if that's not you, use "Your database console" above instead.
db-admin.capstone.uamishub.com

Secrets

Vault
The secrets store behind everything — holds database credentials and API keys for every app. No public UI by design; you manage your app's secrets through Backstage's Secrets tab, not here directly.
